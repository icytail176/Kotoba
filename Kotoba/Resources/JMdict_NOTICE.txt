Kotoba's built-in loanword sidecar primarily preserves the source-form field
from the attributed egg rolls / anki-jlpt-decks vocabulary data. JMdict is used
only as a conservative supplement when it contains an explicit, unambiguous
lsource. The generator and its matching rules are stored in
Scripts/generate_loanword_etymology.py.

The テレビ mapping comes directly from egg rolls' tagged source-form field
(television); it is not inferred from a JMdict gloss or hardcoded in the app.
